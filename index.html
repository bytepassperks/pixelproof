FAQ

Plain answers before you buy.

Do my images get uploaded?

No. PixelProof has no image upload endpoint. Processing runs in your browser; the licensing Worker only handles checkout and licence validation.

Can I hit a specific file size?

Yes. Target-size compression searches quality separately for every image. If the byte budget is impossible at the current dimensions, PixelProof says so and can optionally reduce dimensions.

What metadata is removed?

The Metadata tool shows readable EXIF fields, including camera, timestamps, software, and GPS presence. You can strip all metadata or keep copyright and orientation when exporting JPEG.

Do folder paths survive?

Folder inputs preserve their relative paths inside the downloaded ZIP. Browser support determines whether direct folder selection is available; ZIP remains the portable fallback.

Can I share a workflow?

Yes. Saved settings still stay local, and named pipelines can be exported as JSON and imported by another PixelProof user.

Does PixelProof work offline?

After the app shell is installed or cached, the workbench can load without a network connection. Model downloads for background removal still require network access before first use.

Is background removal automatic?

No. It is click-to-select. You choose the subject, and the browser refines that selection into continuous alpha. People, animals, and products are the strongest cases.

Is face blur automatic?

No. Face blur is assisted manual review. You draw or adjust every privacy box and must explicitly confirm the reviewed boxes before export. It does not claim to catch distant or group faces automatically.

What about glass and transparent objects?

They remain a known weakness. A single photograph often does not contain enough information to separate glass, reflections, refraction, and the background reliably.

Can I process unlimited file sizes?

No. Browser memory and decoded image dimensions are real ceilings. PixelProof rejects images above its configured pixel limit rather than pretending a tab has infinite memory.

What formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC/HEIF input. HEIC is decoded locally in a separately loaded LGPL worker and then flows through the same tools; PixelProof does not encode HEIC. AVIF is not shipped.
